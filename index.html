The Shop

Millions of years,
one small storefront.

"Come and visit us in downtown St. Augustine — we offer a wide collection of fossils, minerals, artifacts, and much more."

Prehistoric is a cabinet of curiosities hiding in plain sight along one of the oldest streets in the country. Inside, you'll find shark teeth pulled from Florida rivers, crystal and mineral specimens cut from the earth's crust, fossils that outdate the coastline outside the door, and the occasional artifact that doesn't fit neatly into any category at all.

Stop in, ask questions, and hold something that's been around a lot longer than St. Augustine has.
